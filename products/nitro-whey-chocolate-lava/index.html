The Power of Creapure® Infused Whey

Kinex Nitro Whey is a premium blend whey protein designed for athletes who demand maximum strength and faster post-workout recovery in a single shake. Each serving delivers 23g of high-quality protein combined synergistically with 3g of Creapure® Creatine Monohydrate.

Creapure® is synthesized in Germany under pharmaceutical conditions to eliminate harmful byproducts like DCD and DHT. By pairing Creapure® with fast-acting whey peptides, muscle cells receive both the amino acid building blocks for protein synthesis and the phosphocreatine substrate required for explosive anaerobic power during high-intensity lifting sessions.
