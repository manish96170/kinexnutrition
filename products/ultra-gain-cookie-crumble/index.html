Precision Hypertrophy Nutrition

Kinex Ultra Gain is a premium mass gainer formulated for individuals seeking convenient, high-quality nutrition to support calorie intake, muscle development, and active lifestyles. Each serving provides 15g of protein and 380 calories, combined with 56g of quality carbohydrates to deliver substantial nutritional support throughout the day.

Hard-gainers often struggle to consume enough whole-food calories to maintain a positive caloric balance. Ultra Gain solves this by pairing clean energy carbohydrates with an advanced multi-stage protein matrix and 3g of Creapure® German Creatine Monohydrate to power cellular ATP regeneration and accelerate tissue recovery.
