The Science of Kinex Elite Whey

Kinex Elite Whey in Cookie Crumble Pop is formulated for athletes and active individuals who refuse to choose between superior sports nutrition and satisfying taste. Each serving provides 24g of protein and 5g of BCAAs, delivering an immediate spike of amino acids to working muscle tissue.

Whey protein contains the highest biological value (BV) of any known protein source, packed naturally with high concentrations of leucine. Leucine directly activates the mTOR complex, triggering muscle protein synthesis to rebuild micro-tears created during resistance workouts.
