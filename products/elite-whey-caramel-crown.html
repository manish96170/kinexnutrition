The Science of Kinex Elite Whey

Kinex Elite Whey is a premium 100% whey protein formulation developed for athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and active individuals seeking high-quality protein nutrition. Each serving provides 24g of protein and 5g of BCAAs, delivering a concentrated source of essential amino acids to support muscle maintenance, recovery, and training adaptation.

Formulated with whey protein isolate, Elite Whey offers a highly bioavailable protein matrix with an optimized amino acid profile and efficient digestibility. Whey protein is naturally rich in leucine, an essential amino acid involved in activating cellular signaling pathways associated with muscle protein synthesis. Its rapid amino acid availability helps support post-exercise protein turnover and physiological recovery following demanding training.

How To Prepare & Consume

Follow these easy steps to prepare a smooth, clump-free protein shake in under 30 seconds.

1

Pour Liquid

Add 6 to 8 fl oz (180–240 ml) of cold water, skim milk, or your preferred beverage into a shaker bottle.

2

Add 1 Scoop

Add 1 level scoop (32 g) of Kinex Elite Whey into the liquid. Close shaker lid tightly.

3

Shake & Enjoy

Shake vigorously for 25 to 30 seconds until completely dissolved. Consume immediately.

Recommended Timing:

Consume within 30 minutes post-workout for optimal muscle recovery, between meals as a high-protein snack, or first thing in the morning to fuel your daily protein intake.
