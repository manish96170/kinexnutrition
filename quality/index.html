Pillar 1: Zero Amino Spiking Policy

What is amino spiking? Many commercial brands exploit nitrogen-testing assays by adding inexpensive, non-proteinogenic amino acids (like glycine, taurine, or creatine) to artificially inflate the total protein count on the label. A product labeled "25g protein" might contain only 15g of real dairy protein and 10g of cheap filler aminos.

The Kinex Guarantee: Every single gram of protein in Kinex Elite Whey, Iso Elite, and Nitro Whey comes 100% from complete, intact dairy whey proteins. We publish full, verified Amino Acid Profiles on our packaging and on every product page — broken down by BCAAs, Essential Amino Acids (EAAs), and total aminos per serving and per 100g.

Pillar 2: Certified Creapure® German Creatine

Creatine monohydrate is the most researched sports supplement in the world, but lower-grade creatines often contain byproducts like dicyandiamide (DCD) and dihydrotriazine (DHT).

Kinex partners with Creapure®, synthesized in Trostberg, Germany under strict pharmaceutical GMP standards. Creapure® guarantees 99.99% pure creatine monohydrate with undetectable contaminant levels, ensuring clean cellular ATP replenishment, explosive training power, and accelerated recovery.

Pillar 3: Fortified Digestive Enzyme Complex

A common complaint among athletes consuming high daily protein intake is digestive discomfort, bloating, or incomplete absorption.

All Kinex protein powders are fortified with 200mg to 400mg of specialized digestive enzymes per scoop. These enzymes rapidly hydrolyze protein peptide bonds into bioavailable amino acids and di/tri-peptides, enhancing intestinal uptake and ensuring zero bloat or stomach heaviness.
